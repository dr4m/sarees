FAQ

Pure Kanjivaram silk and handwoven Banarasi brocade sarees remain the most celebrated weaves across India for weddings, milestone ceremonies, and heirloom gifting.

Pochampally (Bhoodan Pochampally) is world-renowned for its GI-tagged double Ikat weaves, alongside Gadwal and Narayanpet for their signature cotton-silk temple borders.

Heirloom Kanjivaram sarees woven with pure 24-karat gold-dipped silver zari, hand-spun Paithani, and intricate Patan Patola double Ikat sarees command the highest value due to months of master loom artistry.

An unstitched drape typically measuring 5.5 to 6.5 yards, featuring a main body field, contrasting woven borders (korvai or zari), an ornate pallu end-piece, and a matching blouse piece.

A designer saree blends traditional Indian handloom textiles with contemporary silhouettes, pre-stitched pleats, custom hand-embroidery like Gota Patti or zardozi, and couture blouse pairings.

Hyderabad is celebrated for Pochampally Ikat silks, royal Nizami Khada Dupatta ensembles, and finely embellished Gota Patti & zardozi bridal sarees curated at VENKEY.
